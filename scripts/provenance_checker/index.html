// User Guide
HOW TO USE IRIS
01
Find a Video to Analyze
Navigate to any video on X (Twitter), YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, or any platform. Copy the full URL from your browser's address bar or use the share/copy link option.
02
Paste the URL into IRIS
Click the input field at the top of the page and paste your video URL. Click DETECT or press Enter to begin the multi-stage pipeline.
03
Watch the Pipeline Execute
IRIS shows a real-time progress trace — metadata check, video type detection, deepfake analysis. If a fast-exit is triggered, later stages are automatically skipped.
04
Read the Verdict
The result panel shows the final verdict — GENUINE, FAKE, or AI CONFIRMED — with a confidence score and full pipeline trace.
05
Request an Explanation
Click EXPLAIN FURTHER to get a plain-language breakdown of why the verdict was reached. This fires only on demand to conserve processing.
06
Analyze Another Video
Clear the input, paste a new URL, and run again. Each analysis is fully independent. Use IRIS repeatedly to fact-check videos before sharing or reporting.
// Verdict Reference
WHAT THE VERDICTS MEAN
GENUINE
This video is real
No signs of manipulation were found. The faces, voices, and movements all appear natural and unaltered.
FAKE
This video has been manipulated
A person's face or voice has been digitally swapped or altered. Do not share or trust this video as genuine.
AI CONFIRMED
This video was made by AI
The entire video was generated by an AI tool like Sora or Veo3. No real person or footage was used.
SKIPPED
No deepfake check needed
The video is a cartoon, screen recording, or has no real human face — so a deepfake check doesn't apply here.
